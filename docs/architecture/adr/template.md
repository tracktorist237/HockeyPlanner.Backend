# ADR-NNN: Decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Milestone: MNN
- Owners: names or GitHub handles
- Related issues/debt: links or IDs

## Context

Describe the confirmed problem, constraints and exact code/config evidence.

## Decision

State the decision precisely, including boundaries and non-goals.

## Alternatives considered

List realistic alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Describe positive consequences, trade-offs, operational impact and migration or
compatibility concerns.

## Verification

List automated tests, manual checks and observable production evidence.

## Rollback or supersession

Describe how the decision can be reversed or which conditions require a new
superseding ADR.
